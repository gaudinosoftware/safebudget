Restoring your subscription

On a new iPhone or after reinstalling, tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen, or in SafeBudget go to Settings, then Subscription. Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

Cancelling or changing your plan

In SafeBudget, go to Settings, then Subscription, then Manage Subscription. You can also open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. For a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com, since Apple handles all payments.

Logging Apple Pay automatically

In SafeBudget, go to Settings, then Apple Pay Logging, and follow the steps to add the Shortcuts automation. If payments stop appearing after an update, open the automation in Shortcuts and add the SafeBudget Log Expense step again.

Your budget on all your devices

SafeBudget syncs through your own iCloud. Sign in to the same Apple Account on each device and make sure iCloud is on for SafeBudget. In SafeBudget, Settings, then Backup & Sync shows whether syncing is working and lets you make a backup.

Privacy

Your budget stays on your devices and in your own iCloud. Read the full Privacy Policy.
