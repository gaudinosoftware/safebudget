Privacy Policy

SafeBudget for iPhone and iPad. Effective September 26, 2026.

The short version

  • Your budget stays on your devices and in your own iCloud. We can't see it.
  • There's no SafeBudget account. We never ask for your name, email or bank login.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell data.
  • Subscriptions go through Apple. RevenueCat helps us check who is subscribed.

Your budget

Everything you enter in SafeBudget, such as transactions, bills, savings goals, categories, pay schedule and profiles, is stored on your device. If iCloud is on, it syncs between your devices through your private iCloud database, which Apple runs and only your Apple Account can open. SafeBudget has no server of its own, so none of it ever reaches us.

Backups

Backups are saved to SafeBudget's folder in your iCloud Drive. Anything you export goes only where you choose to send it. We don't receive a copy of either.

Features that read information

  • Apple Pay logging. If you set up the Shortcuts automation, Shortcuts passes the Wallet notification text to SafeBudget so it can log the payment. That text is read on your device and not sent anywhere.
  • Receipt scanning. With your permission, the camera scans a receipt. The text is read on your device with Apple's Vision and Apple Intelligence. The picture isn't saved.
  • Category suggestions. Suggestions for names and categories come from Apple's on device models, and from how you sorted earlier payments from the same shop. Nothing leaves your device.
  • Importing statements. Files you import are read on your device and aren't uploaded.
  • Bill reminders. Reminders are scheduled on your device. We don't send push messages or marketing.
  • Widget. The widget reads the same data on your device.

Subscriptions

SafeBudget needs a subscription, which you buy through the App Store with your Apple Account. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card or billing details.

We use RevenueCat to confirm whether a subscription is active. To do that, RevenueCat receives a random ID the app creates (not your name or email), your purchase history for SafeBudget, and basic technical details such as your device model, iOS version, app version, country and IP address. It's used only to provide and restore your subscription.

What we don't do

  • We don't connect to your bank.
  • We don't use advertising, analytics or crash reporting services.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or share personal information.

Deleting your data

  • In SafeBudget, go to Settings, then Backup & Sync, and choose Erase All Data.
  • To remove what's synced to iCloud, open the Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud, then Manage Storage, and choose SafeBudget.
  • Deleting the app removes the data stored on that device.
  • To remove your subscription records at RevenueCat, contact us using the address below.

Children

SafeBudget isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
